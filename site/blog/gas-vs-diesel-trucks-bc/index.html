Gas vs Diesel Trucks in BC: Which One Should You Actually Buy?

By Dan · Personal GM Consultant, Coquitlam BC · Updated 2026-10-06

Diesel trucks carry a mythology: torque forever, million-kilometre engines, fuel savings at every pump. Some of that is true, all of it comes with fine print, and the fine print is where buyers get surprised. The honest answer for most Lower Mainland owners is that modern gas V8s (and GM's turbo-gas options) cover what people actually do with trucks, while diesel wins in specific, identifiable situations. Here is how to tell which situation you are in, before you pay the diesel premium.

What diesel does better

  • Sustained heavy towing: grades, long hills, trailers behind you for hours
  • Highway fuel economy under load, where big diesels genuinely shine
  • Engine longevity for true high-kilometre owners (the million-km stories have roots)
  • Strong resale among towing-focused used buyers

What gas does better

  • Lower purchase price, which means a lower financed payment every month
  • Cheaper, simpler maintenance across ownership
  • Cold-morning behaviour and short-trip friendliness for errand-heavy lives
  • Quiet, refined daily driving that passengers notice

The pattern: diesel pays you back with distance and weight. If your towing is occasional and your driving is local, the diesel premium (at purchase, at the pump for DEF, and at service intervals) quietly works against you. If you tow heavy for a living or drive highways for a living, it quietly pays you every week.

The financing tiebreak

Diesel units carry higher prices used, which raises the financed payment on the same truck. When Dan prices a file, the gas equivalent often approves more comfortably for the same buyer, which matters for rebuild and newcomer programs. His shortcut: decide based on what you tow monthly, not what you tow once a year, and let the payment confirm the choice. Both engine types move through the live inventory weekly; ask what is in stock before you commit to a hunt elsewhere.

The verdict, in one line

Tow heavy monthly or drive 30,000+ highway km: diesel. Everyone else: a modern gas V8 or turbo saves money twice, at purchase and at every service.

Frequently asked questions

Are diesel trucks better for towing?

For sustained heavy towing, generally yes: torque delivery and efficiency under load are their strengths. For occasional light towing, modern gas engines handle it comfortably.

Do diesel trucks last longer?

Well-maintained heavy-duty diesels have genuine longevity reputations, but maintenance costs are higher and short-trip city use can work against them. Usage pattern matters more than fuel type.

Is diesel worth it in BC with fuel prices?

For high-kilometre highway drivers and regular heavy towers, often yes. For local driving, the savings rarely offset the purchase premium; run your annual kilometres honestly.

Can I finance a diesel truck with a credit-rebuild program?

Yes, but the higher price raises the payment, which can strain the approval. A comparable gas unit often finances more comfortably; Dan will price both.
