2025 Silverado vs Silverado LTD: What Actually Changed?

By Dan · Personal GM Consultant, Coquitlam BC · Updated 2026-10-06

The Silverado LTD is not a special edition, a trim, or a trade-in promotion: it is a model-year bookkeeping trick that confuses every used-truck shopper. In short, when GM launched the redesigned 2022 Silverado, it kept selling the old body as the 'Silverado Limited' (LTD) for one final year. So the question 'LTD or regular?' is really 'old body or new body?', and that question has a clear price-based answer. Here is what changed, what did not, and when the LTD is genuinely the smarter buy in the Lower Mainland.

Why the LTD exists (30-second version)

When a manufacturer redesigns a truck, plants need a transition year. GM solved it by continuing the previous-generation Silverado into 2022 alongside the new one, naming the old-body truck the Silverado Limited. It was sold new for a single model year, which makes used LTDs easy to spot and often sharply discounted. Nothing about 'Limited' means loaded or rare; it literally means 'the old one, still available'.

What changed on the new-body truck

  • New interior: bigger screens, better materials, the single biggest day-to-day difference
  • Refreshed front end and lighting signatures
  • Updated trim structure, including newer off-road equipment on ZR2
  • Some safety-tech reorganization across trims

What did not change is the part that matters most: the LTD shares the same engines, transmissions, and core towing hardware as the trucks around it. A well-kept LTD drives like the truck it is, a full-size Silverado through and through.

The depreciation angle

Because the LTD reads as a runout model, its used price took the discount early instead of gradually. Buy one now and somebody else already paid the generation penalty. For financing-minded buyers, that lower principal can be the difference between an approval that strains and one that breathes, and it is one of the quiet bargains Dan watches for in the live inventory.

The verdict, in one line

LTD = previous body at a used-price discount. Same engines, older cabin: buy it for value, skip it if you live inside the tech.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Silverado LTD a limited edition?

No. LTD stands for 'Limited' only in the sense of limited-time availability during the 2022 changeover. It is the previous-generation body, not a special trim.

Is the Silverado LTD reliable?

It carries the same engines and transmissions as its generation, so reliability matches any comparable used Silverado. Service history matters more than the badge year.

Does the LTD tow less than the new body?

For equivalent engine and axle configurations, towing figures are effectively the same. Verify the specific unit's package, as with any used truck.

Can I finance a used Silverado LTD with bad credit?

Yes. Lenders price the payment, not the body style, and the LTD's lower price often helps the approval. Dan handles these files weekly in the Tri-Cities and Surrey.
