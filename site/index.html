COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about our inventory and service in Vancouver and the Tri-Cities.

What types of cars and trucks do you sell?

We carry brand new and used cars and trucks, specializing in GMC, Chevrolet, and Buick models. Our inventory includes SUVs, pickups, sedans, and crossovers — all hand-picked and personally inspected. Proudly serving Vancouver, Coquitlam, and the Tri-Cities, BC.

How can I get in touch for pricing?

Call us at 604-735-1396 for pricing, availability, and test drives. We give honest answers with no pressure — just the way it should be.

Where are you located?

Based in Coquitlam, BC, serving Vancouver, Burnaby, Surrey, Port Moody, Port Coquitlam, and the entire Lower Mainland. Call 604-735-1396 for details and directions.

Do you offer trade-ins?

Absolutely — and we pay the best trade-in values in the Lower Mainland. Bring your current car or truck along and we'll work out a fair, transparent value toward your next purchase. Call 604-735-1396 to talk it through.

Can I get financing with bad credit or no credit?

Yes. We have a high approval rate with easy financing for all credit situations — good credit, bad credit, no credit, bankruptcy, or new to Canada with no Canadian credit history. Call 604-735-1396 and we'll find a program that works for you.

Are your vehicles inspected?

Yes — every vehicle is personally inspected before it's listed. Call 604-735-1396 for the full details on any vehicle you're interested in.
