About Dan

Your personal GM consultant at Eagle Ridge GM in Coquitlam.

Who Dan is

DanGM is Dan, a personal GM consultant working inside Eagle Ridge GM (Eagle Ridge Chevrolet Buick GMC) at 2595 Barnet Hwy in Coquitlam. Instead of passing you between a salesperson, a desk manager and a finance office, you deal with one person from your first call to the day you drive away.

Dan works with buyers across Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Burnaby, New Westminster, Surrey and Vancouver, and specializes in the situations a typical showroom rushes past: rebuilding credit, a past bankruptcy or consumer proposal, and newcomers to Canada with no Canadian credit history yet.

How working with Dan goes

  1. A quick conversation. Call 604-735-1396 or send the 30-second form. Dan asks about your budget, how you'll use the vehicle and your credit situation, without judgement.
  2. A short list, not a lot walk. He pulls the vehicles that fit from Eagle Ridge GM's new and used inventory, which you can also browse on this site, updated every hour.
  3. Financing that fits. Dan submits your application to the lenders and programs that match your situation, including newcomer and credit-rebuilding programs, and explains the numbers in writing before you sign.
  4. Your trade-in. If you have a vehicle to trade, it gets a transparent appraisal, including when you still owe money on it.
  5. Paperwork and delivery. Everything is finalized at Eagle Ridge GM, and Dan stays your contact after the sale.

What Dan can help with

  • New and used Chevrolet, GMC and Buick vehicles, plus other makes taken in on trade
  • Car loans for good, rebuilding or bad credit, and after bankruptcy
  • New-to-Canada, work permit and international student financing programs
  • Trade-ins, including vehicles with negative equity

Where and when

Inside Eagle Ridge GM, 2595 Barnet Hwy, Coquitlam, BC V3E 1K9. Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. Call 604-735-1396.

Vehicles are sold and financed through Eagle Ridge Chevrolet Buick GMC, a licensed motor dealer in British Columbia. Financing is subject to lender approval.
